01Overview

Concord Deal is a confidential marketplace and operating platform for business brokerage. Buyers browse broker-reviewed listings and sign an NDA to unlock financials. Sellers can request a valuation. Brokers manage the whole process — from the first CRM record to a closed deal — inside the platform.

It combines CRM workflows, business listings, buyer/seller management, secure document workflows, financial processing and AI-powered automation in one product.

02The problem

Selling a business depends on confidentiality. Owners don't want staff, customers or competitors to know the business is for sale, and financials should only reach buyers who have been qualified and signed an NDA. At the same time, brokers juggle listings, buyer enquiries, documents and many deals at different stages.

Spread across email, spreadsheets and shared folders, it becomes hard to control who has seen what — and easy for deals to stall.

04My contribution

I built the platform end to end — frontend, backend, database and integrations — including:

  • CRM workflows and buyer/seller management for brokers.
  • Business listings and the public marketplace.
  • NDA workflows and secure access to financial data.
  • Document processing — PDF, Excel and CSV — and charts for financial data.
  • AI integrations using Anthropic's Claude.
  • Authentication, the deal pipeline, monitoring and end-to-end tests.

05Key features

Confidential marketplace

Search listings by keyword, category and location, with identities kept private until a buyer qualifies.

NDA-gated financials

Buyers sign an NDA to unlock confidential financials on a listing.

Recast financials

Financial data normalised to show a business's true earning power, with charts.

Instant valuation

Sellers get an indicative value from their industry and earnings, then request a broker valuation.

Broker CRM & deal pipeline

An advisor workspace for managing buyers, sellers, listings and deals.

AI automation

AI features built on Claude, including the platform's AdvisorAI area, plus PDF and spreadsheet processing.

09Engineering decisions

  • Security in the data layer. Row Level Security in PostgreSQL protects confidential financial data at the database level, not only in the interface.
  • Type-safe, full-stack TypeScript across a Next.js application.
  • Tested and monitored. End-to-end tests and production monitoring are part of the stack, not an afterthought.
